# Page Specs — Location Pages

## ⚠️ The most important rule in this entire scope

**A location page that differs from another location page only by the town name is a doorway page.** Google's spam policies name this explicitly, and sites that do it at scale get filtered out of local results or hit with a manual action. This is the single most common way local contractor sites fail.

**The bar: at least 40% of every location page must be prose that could not appear on any other location page.**

Meeting that bar requires real local knowledge. Budget an hour per page. The good news is that this is also exactly where the competitive opening is — the national lead-gen shells currently ranking for these terms have zero local specificity, so a page that mentions the actual road, the actual neighborhood, the actual ridge, and the actual weather beats them on relevance.

---

## Local specificity sources (use 4+ per page)

1. **Real projects in that town** — "We installed a dark bronze standing seam roof on a farmhouse off `{{ROAD}}` in Richlands last spring." Requires the owner to tag photos by town. This is the strongest ingredient by far.
2. **Named geography** — neighborhoods, roads, ridges, creeks, landmarks. Not just the town name.
3. **Housing stock** — what the buildings are actually like. Richlands has a lot of mid-century brick ranches and older coal-camp housing. Tazewell has historic homes near the courthouse. Rural stretches are farmhouses and barns. Different building stock means different roofing conversations, and describing them accurately is unforgeable.
4. **Local weather and terrain** — elevation, snow load, wind exposure on ridges, ice, valley fog, hard freeze cycles. Burke's Garden sits in a bowl at high elevation and gets weather nothing else in the county gets.
5. **Drive time and coverage** — "About twenty minutes from us, so we're out there constantly."
6. **Town-specific practicalities** — permit office, HOA presence or absence, historic district considerations, typical lot access for a materials truck.

## Location page template

```
1. Breadcrumb        Home › Service Areas › Tazewell County, VA › [Town], VA
2. H1                Metal Roofing in [Town], [ST]
3. Intro             150–200 words. UNIQUE. Primary keyword in first 100 words.
4. Hero image        Ideally a real project in or near this town.
5. Quick-answer box  40–60 words, town-specific.
6. Services here     H2 — 5–6 service links, described in local terms
7. Local projects    H2 — 2–4 photos from this town, captioned
8. Why local matters H2 — UNIQUE. Weather, terrain, housing stock.
9. Nearby areas      H2 — 4–6 links to adjacent town pages
10. Local FAQ        H2 — 4–5 Q&As, at least 2 town-specific. FAQPage schema.
11. CTA band
```

**Word count:** 700–1,000. Priority towns (Bluefield VA, Richlands, Pounding Mill, Tazewell) get 1,000–1,200.

**Meta pattern:**
```
Title: Metal Roofing [Town], [ST] | Metal Roofs & Siding | Metal Master Roofing
Meta:  Metal roofing, siding & board and batten in [Town], [ST]. Local, family
       owned, licensed & insured. Free estimates. Call {{PHONE}}.
```

---

## What NOT to do

❌ Same 600 words with the town swapped
❌ "Serving [Town] and surrounding areas" repeated eleven times
❌ Scraped Wikipedia population/history filler — irrelevant to roofing, and obvious
❌ A block listing 40 nearby ZIP codes
❌ Auto-generated combination pages (`/metal-roofing-richlands-va/` alongside `/service-areas/richlands-va/`)
❌ Fake local addresses or phone numbers per town

## Page-by-page briefs

### `/service-areas/` — hub
**H1:** `Where We Work`
**Words:** 500–700. Overview of the footprint, `{{SERVICE_RADIUS}}`, static service-area map image, grouped link list (Tazewell County VA · Nearby Virginia · Southern West Virginia). No Google Maps iframe.

### `/service-areas/tazewell-county-va/` — county hub
**H1:** `Metal Roofing in Tazewell County, Virginia`
**Words:** 900–1,200. The county-level page. Cover the county's geography — the valleys, the ridges, elevation range, Burke's Garden, the Clinch River corridor — and what all of that means for roofs. Link to all Tazewell County town pages. This page can rank for "roofing contractor Tazewell County VA" which is a strong, high-intent term.

### `/service-areas/bluefield-va/` ★ P1
**H1:** `Metal Roofing in Bluefield, VA`
**Unique angles:** ZIP 24605. **Explicitly distinguish from Bluefield, WV** — link across to that page and say plainly that we serve both sides of the line. Elevation makes Bluefield notably cooler and snowier than surrounding lower ground; the "Nature's Air Conditioned City" identity is locally well known and worth a nod. Mix of older established neighborhoods and newer construction. Proximity to the WV line means many residents work across it.

### `/service-areas/richlands-va/` ★ P1
**H1:** `Metal Roofing in Richlands, VA`
**Unique angles:** ZIP 24641, population around 5,100 — one of the larger towns in the county. Clinch River. Coal-era housing stock alongside mid-century ranches. Regional hospital and commercial corridor make it a light-commercial opportunity. River-valley moisture and fog are relevant to roof and fastener longevity.

### `/service-areas/pounding-mill-va/` ★ P1
**H1:** `Metal Roofing in Pounding Mill, VA`
**Unique angles:** Owner specifically named this as a home base — say so. Small unincorporated community, ZIP 24637 area. Route 460 corridor. More rural and agricultural — barns, outbuildings, shops, farmhouses. This is the natural page to lead with pole barns, ag panel roofing, and board & batten on outbuildings.

### `/service-areas/tazewell-va/` ★ P1
**H1:** `Metal Roofing in Tazewell, VA`
**Unique angles:** ZIP 24651, county seat. Historic downtown and courthouse area; older homes where roof appearance matters and where a homeowner may care about matching a historic look — a genuine argument for standing seam. Higher elevation, real snow load. Burke's Garden is nearby and is a distinctive high-elevation bowl with severe weather.

### `/service-areas/cedar-bluff-va/`
ZIP 24609, population around 1,070. Clinch River town, small and tight-knit, adjacent to Richlands. Mix of older homes and rural properties.

### `/service-areas/north-tazewell-va/`
ZIP 24630. Adjacent to Tazewell proper, more residential and newer. Note the relationship to Tazewell and link between them.

### `/service-areas/claypool-hill-va/`
Route 460/19 commercial corridor between Richlands and Tazewell — the county's retail concentration. Strong light-commercial angle: strip retail, shops, small offices.

### `/service-areas/raven-va/`
ZIP 24639. Small community north of Richlands. Rural, coal-country, older housing stock, many outbuildings.

### `/service-areas/bluefield-wv/`
**H1:** `Metal Roofing in Bluefield, WV`
Mercer County, ZIP 24701. Larger than its Virginia twin. **Link across to the VA page and explain the state-line relationship** — searchers are genuinely confused by two Bluefields, and the page that clears it up earns the click. Note: verify the owner is licensed and insured to work in West Virginia before publishing this page or the Princeton and Bramwell pages. `{{WV_LICENSED}}` — West Virginia requires its own contractor license. **This is launch-blocking for all WV pages.**

### `/service-areas/princeton-wv/`
Mercer County seat, ZIP 24740. Larger market, more commercial. Same WV licensing check applies.

### `/service-areas/bramwell-wv/`
Small historic town, ZIP 24715 — known for its coal-baron mansions and historic district. Historic-appropriate roofing is a real and unusual angle here. Same WV licensing check.

### `/service-areas/lebanon-va/`
Russell County seat, ZIP 24266. Verify the owner travels this far.

### `/service-areas/grundy-va/`
Buchanan County seat, ZIP 24614. Steep mountain terrain and notable flood history — relevant to building materials and roofing decisions. Verify travel distance.

## Interlinking between location pages

Each page's "Nearby Areas" section links to its geographic neighbors — genuinely nearby, not a random assortment:

- Bluefield VA → Bluefield WV, Pocahontas, Falls Mills, Bramwell WV, Tazewell
- Richlands → Cedar Bluff, Raven, Claypool Hill, Doran, Tazewell
- Pounding Mill → Claypool Hill, Cedar Bluff, Tazewell, Richlands
- Tazewell → North Tazewell, Claypool Hill, Bluefield VA, Burke's Garden
- Cedar Bluff → Richlands, Pounding Mill, Claypool Hill
- Bluefield WV → Bluefield VA, Princeton, Bramwell
- Princeton WV → Bluefield WV, Bramwell

Every location page also links up to `/service-areas/tazewell-county-va/` and `/service-areas/`.
