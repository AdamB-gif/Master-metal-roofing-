# Page Specs — Service Pages

## The universal service page template

Every service page follows this structure. Depth varies by tier: flagship pages 1,500+ words, P2 pages 800–1,200, P3 pages 600–900.

```
1. Breadcrumb                Home › Services › [Service]
2. H1 + intro                Primary keyword in H1 and first 100 words
3. Hero image                Real project photo of THIS service
4. Quick-answer box          40–60 words answering the core question. Wins featured snippets.
5. What it is / how it works H2 — educational, builds expertise signals
6. Benefits                  H2 — 4–6 items, each with real substance
7. Our process               H2 — numbered steps, sets expectations
8. Options & materials       H2 — profiles, gauges, colors, warranties
9. Cost factors              H2 — honest ranges. See the cost-transparency note below.
10. Where we do this work    H2 — 4–6 town links (the cross-link mesh)
11. Related services         H2 — 3–4 internal links
12. FAQ                      H2 — 5–7 Q&As with FAQPage schema
13. CTA band                 Call + Free Estimate
```

### Cost transparency — read this before writing any service page

Most contractor sites refuse to discuss price. That refusal is why homeowners bounce and call someone else. Every service page should include a real range with real caveats, sourced from what the owner actually charges:

> Most metal roofs we install in this area run between `{{PRICE_LOW}}` and `{{PRICE_HIGH}}` per square foot installed, depending on panel profile, roof pitch, how many valleys and penetrations you've got, and whether the existing decking needs work. A simple gable ranch is at the low end. A steep, cut-up roof with dormers is at the high end. We'll give you an exact number in writing after we measure.

**Do not publish national-average pricing as if it were local pricing.** Get the owner's actual numbers. If the owner won't provide them, use a qualitative framing instead ("a metal roof typically costs more up front than shingles and less over 40 years") — but push for real numbers, because this section converts.

## `/services/metal-roofing/` ★ FLAGSHIP

**Title:** `Metal Roofing Bluefield VA | Metal Roof Installation | Metal Master`
**Meta:** `Metal roof installation across Bluefield, Richlands, Pounding Mill & Tazewell County VA. Standing seam & exposed fastener. Licensed, insured, free estimates. Call {{PHONE}}.`
**H1:** `Metal Roofing in Bluefield, VA and Across Tazewell County`
**Words:** 1,500–2,000 — the deepest page on the site.

**Quick-answer box:**
> A metal roof installed on a home in Tazewell County typically lasts 40 to 70 years, handles the snow and wind we get on these ridges better than asphalt, and can often go on over your existing shingles. Most homes here use either a standing seam panel or an exposed-fastener ag panel, depending on budget and how the house is going to look.

**Required sections beyond the template:**

- **Standing seam vs. exposed fastener** — a comparison table (cost, lifespan, fastener type, appearance, best use). Link to both dedicated pages and to the blog comparison article.
- **Gauges and materials** — 29 gauge vs. 26 gauge, galvalume vs. galvanized, painted finishes and typical paint warranty terms. This is the vocabulary that signals genuine expertise to both readers and search engines.
- **Metal over shingles** — when it works, when it doesn't, what the code and manufacturer requirements are. Link to the blog article.
- **Colors** — reference the color options actually stocked, link to gallery, link to the color blog post.
- **Snow and ice in the mountains** — regionally specific and almost entirely absent from competitor pages. Cover snow guards over doorways and walkways, and why smooth panels shed snow. This section is a genuine differentiator.
- **Warranty** — `{{WORKMANSHIP_WARRANTY}}` plus manufacturer paint/substrate warranties. Do not state a workmanship warranty length until the owner confirms it.

**FAQ (7):** cost per square foot · lifespan · over shingles? · noise in rain · lightning (no, metal does not attract lightning — and it's non-combustible, which is a genuine advantage) · resale value · installation time.

## `/services/standing-seam-metal-roofing/`

**Title:** `Standing Seam Metal Roof Installation | Southwest Virginia`
**H1:** `Standing Seam Metal Roofing`
**Words:** 1,000–1,400

Explain concealed fasteners and why they matter: the panels lock together and the clips are hidden under the seam, so there are no screw holes through the surface to leak in twenty years. Cover snap-lock vs. mechanically seamed, panel widths, seam heights, thermal movement, and why it's the premium choice. Be straight about cost — roughly double exposed fastener up front, but over a 50-year horizon it typically costs less because you're not chasing failed washers and re-screwing the whole roof. Link to the comparison blog post and to exposed fastener.

**FAQ (5):** cost · why more than screw-down · lifespan · residential or commercial · can it go on a low-slope roof.

## `/services/exposed-fastener-metal-roofing/`

**Title:** `Exposed Fastener Metal Roofing | Ag Panel & R-Panel | SWVA`
**H1:** `Exposed Fastener Metal Roofing`
**Words:** 900–1,200

Ag panel / R-panel / corrugated. The affordable metal option: roughly half the installed cost of standing seam. Be honest about the tradeoff — the screws are part of the weather barrier, and washers degrade over time, so plan on a fastener check every several years and a re-screw eventually. Written honestly, this section builds enormous trust; most competitor pages hide it. Cover proper installation (driving screws straight, correct torque, right screw length) and why installation quality matters more here than on any other roof type.

**FAQ (5):** cost · lifespan · do the screws leak · barn or house · re-screwing.

- **Pairing with a metal roof** — board and batten siding + a standing seam or exposed-fastener roof is a coherent whole-building look, and it's the natural upsell. Link to both roofing pages.

## `/services/storm-damage-roof-repair/`

**Title:** `Storm Damage Roof Repair | Wind & Hail | Tazewell County VA`
**H1:** `Storm Damage Roof Repair`
**Words:** 700–900. Confirm offered. Wind, hail, and fallen-limb damage; documentation for insurance; working with adjusters; emergency tarping.

⚠️ **Do not promise to handle insurance claims, negotiate with adjusters, or waive deductibles.** Contractors adjusting claims without a public adjuster license is a regulatory problem in many states, and deductible waiving is insurance fraud.
