## The 45-minute interview

The most valuable thing the owner can give this project. Record it and transcribe it — the owner's real sentences will outperform anything written from scratch, and they're the source of the local specificity every location page depends on.

1. How did you get into metal roofing? How long have you been at it?
2. What's the job you're proudest of? Where was it?
3. What do you wish homeowners knew before they call anybody?
4. What's the biggest mistake you see on other people's roofs around here?
5. What's different about roofing in these mountains compared to somewhere flat?
6. Walk me through what happens from the first phone call to the last day on the job.
7. What do you tell somebody who's trying to decide between metal and shingles?
8. What are the most common questions people ask you?
9. Tell me about the towns you work in — what are the houses like in Richlands vs. Tazewell vs. out around Pounding Mill?
10. What kind of weather do you deal with here that people elsewhere wouldn't think about?
11. What do you *not* do?
12. Anything a customer said that stuck with you?

**Questions 9 and 10 are the important ones.** The answers are what make fourteen location pages genuinely distinct instead of doorway pages, and that distinction is the difference between ranking and getting filtered.

## Per-photo information

For every project photo, the developer needs:

- **Town** (Richlands, Bluefield, Pounding Mill, Tazewell, etc.)
- **Type of work** (standing seam, ag panel, board & batten, siding, repair, pole barn)
- **Color**, if known
- **Anything notable** — "went over old shingles," "12/12 pitch," "matched the brick," "church in Claypool Hill"

A photo labeled *"roof"* does very little. A photo labeled *"dark bronze standing seam on a farmhouse in Richlands, 12/12 pitch"* becomes a gallery caption, an alt text, a location page illustration, and a small ranking signal all at once.

## Decisions the owner should make

1. **Domain name** — approve one before anything else. It's on every page, every card, every truck.
2. **Publish an address, or stay a service-area business?** Recommend the latter, especially if the business runs from home.
3. **Name the owner on the site?** Recommend yes. A named person with a photo raises contact rates meaningfully.
4. **Publish pricing ranges?** Recommend yes. It's the most common thing people search for and the thing competitors refuse to answer.
5. **Enable Google Business Profile messaging?** Only if it will actually be monitored.
6. **Who maintains the site after launch** — the developer on retainer, or the owner with a simple handoff doc?
